## Corrections made

Starter-container and saucepan-only ingredient placement; reserved butter, marbling paste, fillings, glazing and frying ingredients; combined weights for egg/milk and vegetable batters; manual-mode yeast placement; delayed salt/yeast in autolyse recipes; source line-break units; missing egg wash; poolish quantities and preparation; alternatives separated; and source methods reordered into cooking order. Recipe-book quantities remain visible, including conditional water adjustments from the manual.
