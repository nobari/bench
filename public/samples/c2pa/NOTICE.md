# Sample files for the Content Credentials inspector

`signed.jpg`, `tampered.jpg`, `expired.jpg`, `identity.jpg`, `chain.jpg`, `video.mp4` and `camera.jpg`
are test fixtures from the c2pa-rs project (https://github.com/contentauth/c2pa-rs, `sdk/tests/fixtures`),
licensed under MIT / Apache-2.0. `ai-generated.png` and `ai-edited.webp` were signed with the c2pa-rs public
test certificate for this site's demo.
